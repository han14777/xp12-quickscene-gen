QuickScene - X-Plane 12 city and airport scenery

1. Unzip this folder anywhere you can write to (for example Documents\QuickScene).
   Generated scenery and jobs are stored inside this folder.
2. Double-click QuickScene.exe. A console window opens and the map UI opens in your browser
   (http://127.0.0.1:8765/). Keep the console window open while you use the UI; close it to quit.
3. On first start, DSFTool is downloaded automatically from Laminar Research (files.x-plane.com)
   into the tools folder. X-Plane 12 is found automatically. Both can be changed under
   "Local settings" in the UI.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 x64, X-Plane 12, an internet connection (map tiles, OpenStreetMap data).
Nothing else needs to be installed.

Command line use: QuickScene.exe --help
